# Battalions in Space — large-scale team shooter (Unity 6)

Unity 6000.0.62f1 LTS · Built-in render pipeline · legacy Input Manager (+ gamepad axes) · Windows target (Android builds too) · MCP for Unity bridge.
All IP is original: four factions, their soldiers, heroes, ships, planets and the galaxy. Mechanics follow the 2005-era team shooters (command posts, tickets, heroes earned by points, class awards, space battles with boarding, a galactic campaign). Never use names, characters, designs or sounds from any existing franchise.

## Run
Open `Assets/Scenes/Main.unity` (empty) and press Play: `Game` boots itself (`[RuntimeInitializeOnLoadMethod]`) and builds everything in code — camera, sun, menus, battles. Nothing is authored in the scene and there are no art assets: models come from `MeshKit`, sounds/music from `Sfx`.
Source: https://github.com/darkmage1000/Battalions-in-space (public, branch `master`; builds, Library and preview PNGs are git-ignored, so the repo is code + settings only).
Menu **Battalions > Setup Project** (also runs on load): scene + build settings, player settings, gamepad axes. **Battalions > Build Windows** → `Builds/Windows/BattalionsInSpace.exe`; **Build Android** → `Builds/Android/BattalionsInSpace.apk`.
Build self-test: `BattalionsInSpace.exe -autotest -screen-fullscreen 0` plays every map 12 s with the player spawned, opens a campaign, writes `%USERPROFILE%/AppData/LocalLow/Battalions Studio/Battalions in Space/autotest.txt` and quits.

## Testing in the editor (MCP)
The editor does not tick Play mode while it is in the background. Use `BIS.EditorTools.BattleTest` from `execute_code`: `Start(space, map, f0, f1)`, `Step(seconds)` (frames of 0.02 s via `EditorApplication.Step`, ~60 s of game per call max or the MCP call times out), `SpawnPlayer(cls, ship)`, `Place(pos, look)`, `Report()`, `Watch(seconds, reset)` + `WatchReport()` (per post: seconds being taken / contested / foes near; every change of hands; each squad's order and distance), `MapShot(map)` (Previews/Maps/<name>.png: minimap beside the bot walk grid), `PostEscape()`. The first call right after entering Play mode often does nothing — make a warm-up call. Screenshots: `manage_camera screenshot` with `output_folder: "Previews"` (git-ignored). Stop Play mode before recompiling.

## Architecture (Assets/Scripts, namespace BIS)
- **Core/** `Game` (bootstrap, menus backdrop, `StartBattle`), `Battle` (one battle: teams, `Participant`s, spawning, tickets + bleed, points, hero unlock, class awards, win rules, results; `Bonus` table for Conquest), `Ctl` (all input: keyboard/mouse + Xbox pad via `PadLX..PadDY` axes) + `Settings` (PlayerPrefs), `Mats` (materials from the three shaders in `Resources/Shaders`), `MeshKit` (flat-shaded vertex-colour shapes; colour alpha 0 = glows), `Sfx` (synthesised SFX + march music, pooled 3D sources), `Fx` (two particle systems fed with Emit), `AutoTest`.
- **Data/** `Defs` (WeaponDef / ClassDef / HeroDef / VehicleDef / FactionDef), `Factions` (the four factions + weapon templates `W`), `Maps` (4 land maps with post layouts and `MapFeature` layouts, 3 space maps, themes).
- **World/** `LandWorld` (height-field terrain in 64 mesh-collider chunks, themed props with primitive colliders, outposts, posts + vehicle pads, sky/fog, minimap texture) + `LandWorld.Layout` (the designed features: ridges, roads, rivers / lava / gulches, bridges, lakes, craters, fords, forests, towns, boulder fields), `CommandPost` (neutralise → capture, vehicle pads and respawn; `SpawnableFor(team)` = owned and no enemy standing in it).
- **Units/** `Unit` (soldier/hero: CharacterController movement, heat/ammo weapons, equipment, specials, hero abilities, death), `SoldierRig` (part model per body style + procedural animation), `Combat` (layers, `IDamageable`, `Participant`, `Shots` = every projectile drawn with `Graphics.DrawMesh`), `Gear` (mines, detpacks, supply crates, pickups), `PlayerControl` (input → unit/vehicle, cameras, spawn/dead/pause flow).
- **Vehicles/** `Vehicle` (ground hover vehicles, gunships, starfighters; seats: driver / gunner / passengers; lock-on, repair pulse, landing), `VehicleModels` (per-faction code-built models).
- **Space/** `CapitalShip` (box-built hull with a real hangar + interior corridors, `Subsystem`s: shield, 2 engines, 7 turrets, hangar control, life support, reactor; hull falls mostly through systems), `SpaceWorld` (two ships 1300 m apart, sky, planet / nebula / asteroids).
- **AI/** `BotBrain` (land infantry, drivers, gunners, gunship pilots, space jobs Dogfight / Bomb / Board / Escort / Defend / Sabotage, ship interior nav graph), `Commander` (squads and their orders), `Nav` (land walk grid + A* for infantry and ground vehicles; bots move through `BotBrain.Toward`).
- **Conquest/** `Campaign` (galaxy of 12 worlds + hyperlanes, owners, fleets, credits, bonuses, class unlocks, AI turns, auto-resolve, JSON save `conquest.json`), `GalaxyView` (3D map).
- **UI/** IMGUI in a virtual 1080-high space: `UI` (helpers), `HUD` (battle HUD, radar, spawn screen, map, scoreboard, pause, results), `MenuUI` (+ partial `ConquestUI`).

## Rules of thumb
- Layers by number only: 8 Ground, 9 Unit, 10 Vehicle, 11 Hull (`Layers`). Friendly fire is off; damage goes through `Shots.Deal` (applies `vsVehicle`, bonuses, hit markers).
- Capital ship local frame: bow +x, hangar opens on +z toward the enemy, hangar floor at y = 0. Interior nav nodes in `CapitalShip.nodes` / `links`.
- Bots set one-shot inputs (swap/equip/ability/jump) and `Unit.Tick` clears them.
- New content = add to `Factions` / `Maps` / `Galaxy.Planets`; models follow `FactionDef.body`.
- Map layouts are `MapFeature`s in the same -1..1 space as posts (x0.72 of the half size = 230 m on the 640 m maps). Ridge / river widths and heights are metres; area radii (forest, town, boulders, lake, crater, ford) are in map units. A road that crosses a ridge or crater rim cuts a pass; one that crosses a river / lava / gulch gets a bridge automatically. Keep features ~35 m+ from post centres (posts flatten a 34 m circle). Check a change with `BattleTest.MapShot(map)`.
- Anything that stands or drives uses `Battle.GroundY` (= `LandWorld.Top`, which includes bridge decks), never `Height` alone. `LandWorld.LiquidAt` tells water (wading x0.55, deep x0.35) from lava (damage).

Quickplay + Galactic Conquest. Windows build passes `-autotest`.
- Checked in the editor: land battles (captures, bleeding, vehicles used by bots), hero change (H), space battles (bombers kill shields/engines, ~7 min to a ticket win), bot boarding → reactor sabotage with detpacks, player landing in the enemy hangar and getting out, conquest invasion → battle → capture, a 60-turn auto-resolved campaign to a winner.
- Touch controls (`Core/TouchInput.cs`): floating move stick left, drag-look right, buttons; on automatically on phones, Options > "Touch controls" forces them on a PC touch screen. Menus are IMGUI, so taps work as clicks. Android: IL2CPP ARM64, `Battalions > Build Android`.
- Aim assist (`Units/AimAssist.cs`, used in `PlayerControl.Assist/Magnet` and the flyer branch of `VehicleTick`): friction + view pull + shot magnetism, line of sight required, most centred / nearest target; Settings.AimAssist Off/Low/Medium/High (default Medium) for touch and gamepad, mouse only with Settings.MouseAssist. Starfighters: wider convergence cone and a stick nudge. Crosshair turns orange on an assist target.
- **Playtest pass 2 (2026-11-06):** Options > Controls picks the input device (Auto / Keyboard + mouse / Gamepad / Touch screen; `Settings.Device`, `Settings.Current`); the options shown and aim assist follow it (touch = 1.6x cones and pull, mouse = only with "Aim assist with the mouse too"). Aim assist and touch auto-fire stop at each gun's `WeaponDef.EffectiveRange` (rifle 90, repeater 60, pistol 55, shotgun 22, sniper 320...). The player's shots: spread x0.45 touch / 0.55 pad / 0.7 mouse (x0.6 more with an assist target), 0.2 m sphere-cast bolts. "Show aim and hit statistics" overlays hits / shots, how far the last miss passed an enemy, and the assist target. Touch layout (`TouchInput.Layout`) shows only what fits the moment (foot / driver / gunner / pilot), a second FIRE on the left, FIRE also aims while held, full stick push runs, auto-fire on by default.
- Squad AI (`AI/Commander.cs`): bots in squads of 4; each side splits squads into assault (main target), flank (a second post, via a side waypoint), counter-attack (posts being taken or lost < 45 s ago) and defend (front-line posts); re-planned every 10 s and on every capture; a stalled main push (60 s) rotates to another post (never onto enemy home bases, which are sorted last). Attack waves gather at a staging point 70 m out until 60% of the squad is there, then go in; a spent wave regroups. Squads on the move only fight within 45 m or when shot. Bots spawn at the friendly post nearest their squad's target (assault squads prefer posts with an idle vehicle) and grab vehicles more often.
- Space calmer: craft speed x0.75, turn x0.85, health x1.45, guns x0.62, missiles x0.75, turrets 22 dmg at 1.3/s to 650 m, 6 s launch shield (and turrets ignore launching craft), at most 8 craft per side, bots fire in a tighter cone, broadsides every 6-10 s, shield generators 18000.
- Posts keep their capture area clear: crates sit just outside the cover ring, the bunker and tower stand outside it in the direction furthest from the vehicle pads. Soldiers vault waist-high obstacles automatically (`Unit.Move`, low ray hits / head ray clear). `BattleTest.PostEscape()` walks a soldier out of every post in 12 directions and lists where it stopped.
- **Squads, spawn rules, designed maps (2026-11-06, user: "break teams into squads, spawn on each other in the field if safe... AI not able to steal cap points" + "more creative maps with more defined paths... mountains, buildings, large forests, big rocks, bridges"):** measured first with `Watch`: posts barely changed hands because (1) defenders spawned straight into a post that was being taken, (2) attackers trickled 120-200 m from far posts, and (3) 19 of 29 living bots were snipers - attacking snipers stood still and outlived everyone. Now: no spawning at a post an enemy stands in (`CommandPost.SpawnableFor`; the spawn screen shows it "(attacked)" and the map flashes it). The player is the first member of squad 1 of their side (`Commander` squads include the player). HUD: squadmates in green on the radar, map and name tags (to 150 m), the squad's order under the radar, the spawn screen lists posts + squad with reasons. Snipers in an attacking squad advance with it until 55 m from the objective; bots pick the sniper class 9% (was 14%). Maps were redesigned around routes (`Maps.cs` features): Harrak = a long mesa with two canyon passes, a north loop lane, a gulch with two bridges, a mining town; Elos = a river with three bridges and a ford, seven dense forests, a mill town; Frostfall = a great ridge with one pass at Ridge Relay, a north wall, south cliffs with a long flank road, a frozen lake; Cinder = two lava rivers crossed only on bridges, a crater rim round the Caldera, the Obsidian Spire, foundry towns. Ground vehicles can't climb anything steeper than ~37 degrees; lava burns soldiers (14 / 0.25 s) and vehicles. Measured over ~150 s per map with 16 a side: posts changed hands 3-5 times on every map (Harrak 4, Elos 2 captures + attacks on 3 more, Frostfall 3, Cinder 5), no idle bot away from a fight, `PostEscape` 0-2 of 72 stuck (parked vehicles only).
